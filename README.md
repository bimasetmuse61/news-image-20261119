# news-image-20261119

Sum up the world news of Thursday 19 November 2026 in one image: a
newspaper front page, "THE WORLD — THURSDAY 19 NOVEMBER 2026".

Built for [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261119).

Stories (all reported on 19 November 2026):

- **Lead — Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen.** The three leaders
  hold a trilateral meeting at the APEC summit in Shenzhen; Xi hugged
  Trump on the tarmac as he arrived for the talks on Thursday.
- **The ICJ rules on Myanmar** — the World Court delivers its verdict
  today in The Gambia's genocide case over the Rohingya, seven years
  after it was filed. The court has never found a state guilty of
  genocide.
- **Ukraine strikes the Kerch Bridge again**, for a second night
  running, a Russian official said.
- **Cyclone Akvan closes in** on the Andhra coast, landfall due Friday
  20 November.
- **A Van Gogh sells for a record $190.4 million** after a row over its
  Nazi history.
- **Also today:** GTA VI begins its global rollout; NASA's Voyager 1
  reaches one light-day from Earth; the EU proposes a 45 billion euro
  Ukraine loan; Peltola wins in Alaska.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`). The image was drawn
in the build session with a hand-written PNG encoder and bitmap font;
the program is deterministic and makes no network calls.
